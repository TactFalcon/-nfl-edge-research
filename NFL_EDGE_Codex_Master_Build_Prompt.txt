## 13. SPORTSBOOK MARKETS, PRICING, CASH-OUTS, AND DECISION RULES

Implement exact mathematically tested American odds handling, including implied breakeven rates, plus/minus odds, fractional payouts, commissions/fees where relevant, two-sided de-vig when both sides are known, pushes/voids, alternative milestones, anytime TD, game moneyline/spread/total, SGP correlated legs, and different settlement rules across FanDuel, Kalshi and other user-provided sources. **FanDuel TO WIN is profit; payout includes principal.** Do not conflate either.

- Positive American +a -> implied `100/(a+100)`; negative -a -> implied `a/(a+100)` with `a = abs(odds)`.
- A $stake win at +a returns stake + stake*a/100; -a returns stake + stake*100/a.
- `EV = P(win)*net_profit_if_win - P(loss)*stake` with explicit push/void treatment and fees. This is NOT meaningful if probabilities are unvalidated.
- `cashout_hold_threshold = offered_cashout / possible_total_payout` **only if no other residual partial outcomes and full ticket pays all-or-nothing**; adjust for partial cash-outs/refunds, fees and conditional results otherwise. Compare the threshold to the JOINT probability all remaining legs settle successfully, not one highlighted player leg unless all others are already final.

For each market calculate current price, timestamp, implied rate, optional de-vig benchmark, model point and uncertainty interval, baseline model, price sensitivity, conservative or plausible EV range, counter-case, and PASS/VALUE CANDIDATE. Distinguish *high hit probability* versus *positive value*: a 75% over at -500 is not attractive merely because it is likely.

**Qualification gates:** missing price => `INSUFFICIENT PRICE / NO EV`; stale or conflicting injury => `PASS or CONDITIONAL`; missing essential route/coverage data => do not invent an edge; model not validated for market => `RESEARCH-ONLY`; model interval crossing break-even after errors/uncertainty => `MARGINAL/PASS`; multiple independent robust model checks plus confirmed price may merit `VALUE CANDIDATE` (not a command to wager). Require explicit explanation of what would invalidate a proposed advantage.

**PASS without apology.** If the slate has no qualifying prices, answer that there are none. Do not force three legs to hit +200, add an arbitrary touchdown to reach 3x, or identify a bet for each matchup. Rank evaluated candidates by *robustness* not point-estimate EV. The output should show candidates rejected and WHY.

**SGPs:** never multiply marginal hit rates without testing correlation.
